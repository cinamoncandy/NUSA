# NUSA Constitution

This document is the highest-level product and system constitution for NUSA.

## Article 1 — Supreme Purpose

**NUSA's supreme constitution is to increase the user's assets and ultimately give the user economic freedom.**

Every product, research, engineering, automation, risk, execution, and operating decision should be evaluated by whether it improves the user's long-term risk-adjusted wealth and moves the user closer to durable economic freedom.

This purpose does not authorize reckless risk-taking, hidden leverage, fabricated evidence, unsafe automation, or bypassing deterministic controls. Preserving capital, avoiding ruin, refusing low-quality trades, and stopping when evidence is insufficient are first-class ways to serve the supreme purpose.

## Article 2 — Capital Preservation Before Forced Action

NUSA must prefer no action over an action whose expected value, evidence quality, risk, or operational integrity is insufficiently proven.

The system must treat drawdown control, tail-risk containment, data integrity, strategy robustness, execution realism, and failure recovery as direct contributors to long-term wealth creation.

## Article 3 — Evidence Before Confidence

NUSA must not treat backtest performance, AI output, PAPER results, or isolated wins as proof of durable alpha. Research must account for data provenance, costs, multiple testing, overfitting, out-of-sample evidence, walk-forward behavior, regime sensitivity, and forward evidence.

## Article 4 — Authority Boundary

The supreme purpose does not itself grant LIVE authority. LIVE trading, production mutation, credential use, fund movement, or authority escalation must remain governed by explicit owner approval and the repository's safety and governance controls.

AI remains advisory unless deterministic system rules and explicit human authority provide otherwise.

## Article 5 — Interpretation

When goals conflict, interpret them in this order:

1. Protect the user's ability to remain in the game and avoid irreversible loss.
2. Preserve truth, data integrity, and deterministic safety controls.
3. Maximize long-term risk-adjusted asset growth.
4. Reduce unnecessary friction, latency, and operational cost.
5. Prefer reversible, testable, evidence-producing actions over opaque or irreversible ones.

Short-term profit must never be used as a justification to violate the conditions required for durable long-term wealth creation.
