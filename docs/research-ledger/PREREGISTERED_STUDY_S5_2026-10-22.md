# Pre-registered study S5: order-book imbalance, 15-minute horizon (design v6, 2026-10-22)

Status: DESIGN ONLY. No script, no data read. Committed to the repository before any order-book row was examined for this signal. The older order-book study (WO-RESEARCH-20261014-ORDERBOOK-STUDY) contains imb5 among its features, so S5 is not blind to the idea; this design is frozen before that study prints (2026-10-29T02:00Z), so S5 has seen none of its output. PAPER_ONLY,
liveAuthority=NONE, productionMutationAllowed=false, aiAuthority=ZERO_AUTHORITY. A result is research evidence; a PASS only qualifies a
forward SHADOW test, never a promotion. Nothing below may change after the freeze (see Freeze).

## Why this study

S2-S4 decide once a day, so one cohort is one sample and a verdict needs about 30 days. The order-book collector sees ~290 KRW
markets every minute, so a decision every hour gives 24 cohorts a day. S5 asks one narrow question that is fast to answer: **does the
top-5 depth imbalance predict the next 15 minutes of mid-price return across markets, and if so, is it larger than what taking liquidity
costs?** Expected outcome, stated plainly: the gross effect may exist but is likely to be a few basis points, below taker cost, so the
net test probably fails. The gross answer still matters: it tells S3/maker work whether there is any signal worth executing cheaply.

## Data (what exists, and the limit it sets)

Books come from `research_orderbook_samples` UNION `research_orderbook_study_rows` (scripts/lib/orderBookSource.js). After the 14-day
prune only minutes 58, 59, 0 of every hour, minutes 1-10 of the 4-hourly hours and minutes 43-45 of every hour are kept. S5 therefore
uses only books at minutes 43-45 and 58-0. This needs PR #2854 (migration 032) deployed on the server before the first training-day rows reach 14 days (rows of 2026-11-22 are pruned from the live table on 2026-12-06), else
the early training days are gone; the study then ends INSUFFICIENT_DATA through the eligibility and 303-cohort rules, and a re-run cannot bring the deleted days back (declared, not repaired).

## Design

- **Spans (UTC):** eligibility training 2026-11-22T00:00 .. 2026-12-06T00:00 (14 days, 336 decision hours; used only for eligibility). **Holdout 2026-12-06T00:00 .. 2026-12-20T00:00 (14 days, 336 decision hours)**, which is after 2026-12-05T00:00Z as the S2-S4 rule "any later registration must use data after 2026-12-05" requires; no S5 data overlaps the S2-S4 holdout 11-05..12-05 as a test. The training span lies inside that holdout and is used only to decide which markets are eligible, never to test or tune a signal; S5 is frozen long before the S2-S4 verdict prints. First verdict printed from 2026-12-20T02:00Z.
- **Decision times:** every hour at hh:45. Entry book at t = the last sample in (t - 2 min, t]. Exit book = the last sample in
  (t + 13 min, t + 15 min]. Horizon is 15 minutes from the decision time.
- **Book at a time (no look-back):** the book at t is the LATEST sample in the window; if that sample is not valid the market has no book at t (no fall-back to an earlier valid sample). The entry window is (t - 2 min, t], the exit window is (t + 13 min, t + 15 min]; a market whose exit sample is invalid has no exit book. The last decision (2026-11-25T23:45Z) exits at 2026-11-26T00:00Z exactly, which is allowed. Rows are never ties after the (market, stamp) dedup.
- **Valid book:** best_bid > 0, best_ask > 0, best_bid <= best_ask, bid_size_5 + ask_size_5 > 0, stamp inside the window (open at the start, closed at the end). Rows are deduplicated by (market, stamp) before use, so the row count and sha256 are stable. Mid = (best_bid + best_ask) / 2.
- **Eligible market** (training data only): has, in the SAME decision hour, a valid entry book and a valid exit book in at least minimumCount(n) = ceil(9n/10) = 303 of the n = 336 training decision hours (14 days x 24) (integer arithmetic, as scripts/lib/preregisteredStudyStats.js `minimumCount`) AND the UPPER median of its entry-book spreads (bp) over those training decision hours is <= 20 (no values = not eligible). Definitions used everywhere below, per cohort c: the ranked set R_c = eligible markets with a valid entry book at t; the benchmark set B_c = the members of R_c that also have a valid exit book. A pick is a member of R_c. Pick gaps = picks without a valid exit book; benchmark gaps = members of R_c without a valid exit book (so |R_c| is the benchmark denominator). There is no separate training-day coverage rule: a thin training span leaves too few eligible markets, so cohorts are empty and the 303-cohort rule gives INSUFFICIENT_DATA (fails closed). A cohort is non-empty only if at least 60 eligible markets have a valid ENTRY book at t, else it is empty (excess 0, counted in n, not a down block).
- **Signal:** imb5 = (bid_size_5 - ask_size_5) / (bid_size_5 + ask_size_5) of the valid entry book. One feature, no model, no fitted
  parameter, nothing tuned. Rank the eligible markets with a valid entry book at t (ranking never looks at the exit book) by imb5; ties are broken by the LOWEST market code (bytewise) winning the better position for both S5H and S5L.
- **Picks:** S5H = the top ceil(n/10) (integer: floor((n + 9) / 10), n = ranked markets) by imb5 (high bid-side depth): a momentum hypothesis, bid-heavy books are followed by rises. S5L = the bottom ceil(n/10): a contrarian long-only hypothesis, ask-heavy books are followed by rises (it tests that low-imbalance picks OUTPERFORM; no short position is simulated). Equal weight per pick. Two hypotheses, one cohort each hour.
- **Returns:** gross r_i = mid(exit) / mid(entry) - 1. Benchmark = mean r over B_c.
  Excess_c = mean(r of picks) - benchmark. Taker net of a pick = bid(exit) x (1 - c) / (ask(entry) x (1 + c)) - 1 with c = (fee + slippage) / 10000 per side, the SAME costs as S2-S4: base 5 bp fee + 5 bp slippage, stress 10 bp fee + 10 bp slippage. The spread is inside ask/bid, so the slippage term is deliberately conservative (an extra safety margin, not a double count error).
- **Data gaps:** among ranked markets, a pick without a valid exit book is valued -100% and counted as a gap; a benchmark market without a valid exit book is excluded and counted. Gap limits are pooled over the whole holdout, per hypothesis: pick gaps above 1% of all picks (tighter than S2-S4, because one gap pick of -100% outweighs a few-bp effect), or benchmark gaps above 5% of all benchmark markets, give INSUFFICIENT_DATA (integer tests: pick gaps x 100 > total picks, i.e. above 1%, or benchmark gaps x 20 > sum of |R_c|; both sums run over NON-empty cohorts only. A non-empty cohort whose B_c is empty is treated as an empty cohort everywhere, including the 303 rule). Expected, not a rule: imbalance extremes are thin books and may gap more than the universe, and a gap pick costs -100% against an effect of a few bp, so gaps can only work against a pass and in a direction-dependent way. At least 303 of the 336 cohorts (integer ceil(9 x 336 / 10)) must be non-empty, else INSUFFICIENT_DATA.
- **Unit of inference = 4-hour block** (84 blocks: 00-04, 04-08, ... UTC). A cohort belongs to the block of its decision hour (the 03:45 decision, which exits at 04:00, is in block 00-04). Block value = mean of the Excess_c of its 4 cohorts (empty cohorts contribute 0). Hours inside a block share market state, so cohorts are not independent; blocks are the pre-registered compromise. The lag-1..6 autocorrelation of block values is printed, and the dependence guard is: for each hypothesis, the lag-1 autocorrelation r1 of its 84 gross-excess block values, r1 (0 if the block variance is 0) = sum over t = 2..84 of (x_t - m)(x_(t-1) - m) divided by sum over t = 1..84 of (x_t - m)^2, m = mean, is compared with 0.3. If r1 > 0.3 the outcomes HOLDOUT_PASS and SIGNAL_EXISTS_NOT_TRADABLE of that hypothesis become INCONCLUSIVE_DEPENDENCE (positive dependence only inflates false positives, so SIGNAL_NOT_FOUND is never changed).
- **Tests per hypothesis** (S5H and S5L separately), on block values, with exactly the implementations of scripts/lib/preregisteredStudyStats.js (`studentTUpperP`, `signFlipP`, the sd tolerance 1e-12; sd below it fails the test): one-sided Student t (df = blocks - 1) AND sign-flip permutation (100,000 flips, mulberry32 seed 20261022, same draw order, p = (1 + count) / 100001). **Alpha = 0.05 / 2 = 0.025** (Bonferroni over the two
  hypotheses; they are the only primary tests of S5).
- **Verdict** (precedence, first match wins; S5H and S5L are evaluated together):
  1. DATA_INTEGRITY_FAIL.
  2. WAITING_DATA (before 2026-12-20T02:00Z).
  1b. VOID (a process fact, recorded by hand, takes precedence over everything except step 1): the design hash was not set before 2026-10-29T00:00Z, or the scripts were not reviewed, merged and tested before 2026-12-06T00:00Z.
  3. INSUFFICIENT_DATA for both hypotheses if fewer than 303 of the 336 cohorts are non-empty.
  4. Gross tests of each hypothesis (p < 0.025 on both tests and mean excess > 0 = "gross pass"). If BOTH hypotheses gross-pass, both are `BOTH_TAILS_AMBIGUOUS` (both tails working points to thin books, not direction; never a pass). This step comes BEFORE the per-hypothesis gap, dependence and down-block checks, so no other trigger can let one tail stand alone.
  5. Per hypothesis: INSUFFICIENT_DATA if its gap limit is breached (data defect; may be re-run once as below); else `SIGNAL_NOT_FOUND` if it did not gross-pass; else (it gross-passed, the other did not) INSUFFICIENT_DATA if fewer than 8 down blocks; else INCONCLUSIVE_DEPENDENCE if its r1 > 0.3; else
  - `HOLDOUT_PASS` only if mean base taker net > 0 AND stress taker net > 0 AND the mean GROSS excess block value on down blocks is > 0 with one-sided Student t p < 0.10 (df = k - 1, k = number of down blocks, same sd tolerance; sd below it fails). A down block is a block that has at least one non-empty cohort AND whose benchmark GROSS return is < 0, the benchmark gross return of a block being the mean over its non-empty cohorts of the mean r of B_c (a block with only empty cohorts is not down; its block value is 0 as above). The gross benchmark is used on purpose: the base-cost net of a 15-minute benchmark is negative in almost every block, which would turn the clause into a restatement of the excess test. Known bias, either direction: conditioning on the benchmark return shifts the excess of any pick set whose beta is not 1.
  - `SIGNAL_EXISTS_NOT_TRADABLE` otherwise (gross pass, net or down-block condition failed). Information, not a pass.
  Taker net is the mean over all 336 cohorts of the cohort-mean net of its picks (gap picks -100%, empty cohorts 0); it is a point estimate, deliberately without its own significance test (the gross tests are the inferential gate). Assumptions, all optimistic for the taker: fill of the whole position at the best ask/bid of the same snapshot that produced the signal, no size limit, no latency, no depth impact.
- **Printed (not tested):** mean gross excess in bp, mean taker net in bp (base and stress), block count, sd of block values, minimum
  detectable excess at the used alpha, the fraction of picks that are gaps, the dataset sha256 and row count (same dataset-identity rule
  as the other studies), S5H and S5L excess in the first and the last 7 days of the holdout, the distribution of the realised horizon (exit stamp - entry stamp; the 15 minutes are nominal, 13 to 17 are possible), and the gap-pick share per cohort. Ties of imb5 at the pick boundary: the tie share is printed. The dataset identity is the sha256 over the deduplicated rows actually read (entry and exit windows of eligible markets) in canonical (market, stamp) order.
- **Fails closed:** any stored row stamped more than 5 s after the run time gives DATA_INTEGRITY_FAIL.

## What is deliberately not done

No learned model, no combination of features, no maker/limit-order fill model (that is S3's job; S5 only says whether a signal exists),
no second look at the holdout, no change of the 10% pick size, the horizon or the block length after seeing data. Sensitivity runs
(pick share 5% / 20%, horizon-matched benchmark) may be printed but are outside the decision.

## Freeze and amendments

The design is frozen by a sha256 of this file's exact LF bytes, set as `S5_DESIGN_SHA256` (a constant separate from the S2-S4 `DESIGN_SHA256`; addenda in its own registry) in scripts/lib/preregisteredDesign.js, in a commit after the final design commit and
before 2026-10-29T00:00Z (before the older order-book study prints). The runner refuses to run while the hash is PENDING or the bytes differ. Re-run rule (S2-S4 style, per hypothesis): a hypothesis whose first outcome is INSUFFICIENT_DATA may be re-run ONCE, with the same design hash and code, after a documented data repair; the first outcome is recorded. A re-run recomputes only the breaching hypothesis; the other hypothesis keeps its first-run verdict and its first-run gross results, and step 4 (BOTH_TAILS_AMBIGUOUS) is evaluated against those recorded first-run gross results. Every other verdict stands and a hypothesis is not re-run on the same data. A re-run needs the training days, which the archive keeps 75 days after capture (training days from 2026-11-22 until about 2027-02-05). A missed PR #2854 deployment shows up as INSUFFICIENT_DATA (too few eligible markets, hence too few non-empty cohorts), not VOID. Changes after the freeze are only dated, hashed addenda that change no rule above. The scripts must be reviewed, merged and tested before 2026-12-06T00:00Z, else S5 is VOID.
