Related: `docs/RESEARCH_SIGNAL_SCREEN_DESIGN_2026-11-20.md`, `docs/RESEARCH_PREREG_2026-11-19_CROSSMARKET_MOMENTUM_60M.md`, `docs/RESEARCH_PREREG_2026-11-20_SIGNAL_SCREEN_60M.md`.

## 1. What "all past records" means here
- **Our own trade records: almost none.** The service has run COLLECT_ONLY with orders OFF; the PAPER ledger holds 0 orders and 0 fills, and the permit inventory found 0 calibration and 0 outcome samples (`docs/NUSA_PERMIT_PRODUCER_DESIGN_2026-11-16.md` section 7). There is nothing of our own to learn from.
- **Market history we collected** (extents are **not measured in this document** and must be fixed from counts before anything is read):
  - 1-minute candles: KRW-XRP for about two months (earlier experiments read them). For BTC, ETH, SOL and DOGE the history comes from the **public backfill of 2026-11-19 and later live collection**; the backfill targets up to about 56 days per market (`docs/RESEARCH_MARKET_EXPANSION_DESIGN_2026-11-19.md`, about 80,000 rows; store cap 100,000 rows per market), but its completion was **not measured** (the gap-fill health of 2026-11-20 still showed hundreds of missing minutes per market). The extent is therefore somewhere between about 12 days (what the manifests of 2026-11-20 showed, a window-limited count) and about 56 days.
  - Order-book tops: about 300 markets, **rolling 14-day retention** (configurable up to 90 days) and a per-market sample cap; **books before the cut will be pruned** (data of 2026-11-20 around 2026-12-04) unless retention is raised or a snapshot is taken. The 68 percent coverage figure is the watched market's last 192 hours.
  - Trade ticks: a **count cap per market** (2,000,000), not a time window; the time span is unverified.
- **Already tried on this history:** 811+ rule experiments (0 candidates, XRP) and a trade-flow machine-learning study (failed); the earlier order-book and trade-flow studies read book and tick data of several markets. Mining it again with the same kind of search has a low prior.

## 2. The main risk: a search over history always finds a winner
If many rules are tried on one history, the best one looks good by chance, and more rules make the best look better. A strategy fitted on a few weeks of data is **a hypothesis, never evidence**. The repository's answer is the pre-registered forward test; this design keeps that and uses the history only to **generate and rank candidates**.

## 3. Blindness: which history may be used
- Reserved by pre-registrations: from 2026-11-20T00:00Z candle values of all five markets (cross-market study: nobody reads them before its run), and the signal screen's span from 2026-12-11. **This design reads none of them.** The mining window must **end at 2026-11-20T00:00Z at the latest.**
- Usable for exploration: five-market candles before that cut. BTC, ETH, SOL and DOGE candle values were never read before (this design will read them; it is allowed because they lie outside every reserved span). **XRP is not blind** (earlier experiments), so XRP is **discovery-only** and is never part of a hold-back claim. Book and tick data before the cut are not blind for markets that earlier studies read.
- **No result for a pre-registered rule or signal** (the cross-market top-1 24 h momentum, RET6, RET24, RET72, VOL24, SURGE, RANGE) may be computed or reported from the mining window: a duplicate check is done **by formula, not by outcome**, so that the mining cannot preview the pre-registered hypotheses.

## 4. Protocol (proposal)
1. **Fix the windows from counts**, before any value is read: a discovery window (the older part) and one hold-back window (the newer part, ending at or before the cut). A purge of at least 72 hours (the longest lookback) is left between them and no rule may use data across the boundary; the hold-back is therefore not adjacent to the discovery data. The 72-hour purge and the window sizes are written into the freezing record.
2. **Exploration in the discovery window only**, any rule family, any number of trials, with a **trial log**; the number of trials is a **disclosure only** and does not enter the hold-back test.
3. **Shortlist** at most 5 candidates by a rule written before exploration: highest discovery-window mean net return after the fixed costs of the cross-market study (fee 0.05 percent per side; stress 2 x fee + 5 bp), **with at least 100 discovery trades** and **no two candidates whose trade sets overlap by more than 50 percent** (the lower-ranked one is dropped).
4. **Freeze, then one look.** The shortlist, the PASS threshold and the test are **committed before the look** (and the file records the time and the commit). The test is the one of the cross-market pre-registration section 5 (circular block bootstrap with block 24, B = 10,000, centred null, one-sided) with a Bonferroni correction **over the shortlist size only** (at most 5; selection from the trial log is already paid for by discarding the discovery results). The result is **descriptive**: a filter that lets a candidate into step 5, not a hypothesis test whose p-value is evidence. After a failed look the hold-back window is spent and **the discovery window is not reopened for the same candidates**.
5. **Forward confirmation:** each surviving candidate gets its own pre-registration with a future span; **each writes its own rule** with the same cost model as the cross-market study and the same SHADOW-only meaning of a pass. Nothing is promoted by steps 1 to 4.

## 5. What it can and cannot say (assumptions, not measured data)
- With a 4-day hold-back (about 96 decision hours; this assumes **one always-open position**, one trade per hour) and a trade standard deviation near 0.8 percent, the standard error is about 0.08 percent; with the correction over a shortlist of 5 (alpha about 0.01, z about 2.3) the critical mean net is about 0.19 percent and 80 percent power needs about 0.26 percent per trade. **Smaller edges are invisible.** A hold-back of 20 days (if the history reaches 56 days) would have a standard error of about 0.036 percent and see edges near 0.12 percent: **the extent of the history decides the power and must be measured first.**
- The two windows are within one regime; a rule fitted to that regime can pass the hold-back by regime luck. This is a limit that cannot be removed with the data we have.
- **Calendar:** candidates appear early, but the forward confirmation cannot start before the reserved spans end (the cross-market holdout to 2026-12-10, the signal screen to 2027-01-17); a second screen on a later span starts after that. Mining gains **no confirmation time**, only an earlier candidate list.
- It cannot create evidence of profit. A candidate is as unproven as any other until its forward span is done.

## 6. Overlap and limits
- The signal screen's six signals are frozen; candidates from this design enter a **second** screen on a later span (after 2027-01-17), a separate family.
- A candidate that duplicates a signal of the screen or the cross-market rule is dropped (by formula, section 3).
- No order-book or tick candidate is shortlisted unless its usable-hour share in the windows reaches 90 percent for the markets it uses; and the book and tick rows must exist at freeze time (retention above).

## 9. Implementation (2026-11-20, `scripts/run-history-mining.js`, work order `WO-LEARNING-20261120-HISTORY-MINING-SCRIPT`)

The owner asked to build it ("만들어"). The script implements section 4 and nothing else; it is **not wired** into the Research runner or the diagnosis and **has not been run** on any real data. It reads `research_closed_candles` read-only, places no order and prints aggregates only.

- **Two phases.** `discover` explores the fixed grid in the discovery window and prints the shortlist, the frozen thresholds and a freeze record (with a fingerprint over the grid, windows, shortlist, thresholds and alpha). The freeze record is committed to the repository **before** `look` is run. `look` is the one look: it refuses a changed, foreign or already used freeze record and applies the frozen thresholds unchanged to the hold-back window of the **four blind markets only** (XRP is discovery-only).
- **The grid** (128 trials, fingerprinted): eight signals (RET1, RET3, RET12, RET48, VOLRATIO = 6 h over 72 h realised volatility, CLOSE_VS_MA24, DRAWDOWN24, RUNUP24) x side (enter at or above the q-quantile, or at or below the (1-q)-quantile) x q in {0.8, 0.9} x hold in {1, 4, 12, 24} hours. RET6, RET24, RET72, VOL24, SURGE and RANGE are not in the grid (duplicates by formula of the pre-registered signal screen).
- **Readings made where the design leaves room** (open to the owner's correction before the first run): long only; hourly decisions on hourly closes; one position per market (a rule cannot re-enter before its exit); entry and exit at the hourly close; a missing needed hourly close makes the decision or the trade unusable (never filled, disclosed as `unresolved`); thresholds are quantiles of the pooled discovery signal distribution (all five markets) and are not refitted in the hold-back; cost = round trip 2 x 0.05 percent (10 bp), stress = per side 2 x fee + 5 bp, so 30 bp for the round trip (pre-registration section 2); shortlist = highest discovery mean net, at least 100 trades, at most 5, drop a candidate whose trade set overlaps an already chosen one by more than 50 percent; the look test is the circular block bootstrap (block 24, B = 10,000, centred null, one-sided) over **hourly bins of the hold-back** (trades summed per entry hour, so simultaneous trades of the correlated markets stay together) with alpha 0.05 divided by the shortlist size; a pass needs at least 50 trades, mean net above zero at the base cost **and** under the stress cost, and p below that alpha; fewer than 50 trades or zero variance is not testable and never a pass.
- **Blindness is enforced in code:** every SQL upper bound is below 2026-11-20T00:00Z, the hold-back must end at or before it and lie at least 96 hours after the discovery window, and rows at or after the cut are filtered again after the read. Tests show that rows planted after the cut change nothing.
- **What has to happen before the first real run:** the extent counts (the 2026-11-21 `candle_extent` line) fix the window boundaries; the owner or an independent reviewer reads the readings above; a release puts the script on the host; the run is a read-only host command (or a later wiring PR); the freeze record is committed before `look`.

### 9.1 Changes after the independent review (2026-11-20)
The review found no blindness leak and three P1 points, all fixed with tests: (1) the look had no minimum trade count, so two lucky trades could pass: now at least 50 trades, zero variance not testable; (2) the bootstrap over the trade sequence under-captured the dependence between simultaneous trades of the four markets (anti-conservative): now hourly time bins; (3) the stress cost was read as 25 bp instead of 30 bp and was not part of the pass rule: now 30 bp and part of the rule. Also: the purge is 96 hours (the 72 hour lookback plus the longest 24 hour hold) instead of the 72 hours of section 4; the freeze record carries the source commit sha of the code that produced it (the grid fingerprint binds the grid constants, not the signal formulas).
**Known limits, stated on purpose:** (a) "one look" is enforced by procedure only (the committed freeze record, the owner and the reviewer): nothing in the code can stop a second `look` run, and a later freeze must never reuse a hold-back window that was already looked at; (b) shortlist overlap compares entry timestamps, so rules with different holds of the same signal can be near-duplicates in exposure (this costs power through the 0.05/k split, not validity); (c) the markets are highly correlated, so four markets are far fewer than four independent samples.

## 10. Runbook (wiring, 2026-11-20, work order `WO-LEARNING-20261120-HISTORY-MINING-WIRING`)

The Research runner now has one bounded child (300 s) that runs only the `discover` phase, and the diagnosis shows its aggregate line as `history_mining`. Until the owner commits `deploy/oracle/history-mining-windows.json` it prints `WAITING_CONFIG` and reads no candle value. The steps, in order:
1. **Counts.** Read the `candle_extent` line of the 2026-11-21 diagnosis (rows, first and last close, absent and missing minutes per market). No value is read.
2. **Choose the windows** from the counts: four full UTC hours `discoveryStart < discoveryEnd`, a purge of at least 96 hours, `holdbackStart < holdbackEnd <= 2026-11-20T00:00Z`. The hold-back is as long as the history allows (power, section 5).
3. **Commit the config** `deploy/oracle/history-mining-windows.json` with exactly those four ISO UTC fields (for example `"discoveryStart": "2026-10-01T00:00:00Z"`). An invalid file makes the child fail closed (`HISTORY_MINING_FAILED_CLOSED`); it never blocks Research.
   **Chosen 2026-11-22 (WO-LEARNING-20261122-HISTORY-MINING-WINDOWS)** from the `candle_extent` counts after the deeper backfill (BTC, DOGE, ETH, SOL: 45.7 days from 2026-10-07 08:01-08:05Z; missing minutes BTC 318, ETH 459, SOL 1,329, DOGE 6,394): discovery 2026-10-08T00:00Z to 2026-11-01T00:00Z (24 days), purge 96 h, hold-back 2026-11-05T00:00Z to 2026-11-19T23:00Z (14.96 days). Risk: the hold-back may give fewer than the 50 trades the look needs; the look would then report INSUFFICIENT and the window is spent.
4. **Release.** The config ships with the next release; Research then runs `discover` read-only at its next cadence.
5. **Read `discover`** in the diagnosis `history_mining` line (status, trials, usable hours, eligible and positive-mean rules, grid fingerprint, shortlist ids with n, meanNet, meanStress). The full freeze record (thresholds, windows, source commit, fingerprint) is shown by the diagnosis line `history_mining_freeze` (strictly validated, fixed fields, shortlist ids of the grid shape only, `lookedAt` must be null); copy that JSON unchanged into the repository as the freeze file in step 6 (the `createdAt` field is not part of the fingerprint).
6. **Commit the freeze record BEFORE `look`**, reviewed; a later freeze never reuses a hold-back window that was already looked at.
7. **One look, by the owner's word only.** `look` is never wired, never scheduled and never run by the runner: it is a manual command of `scripts/run-history-mining.js` with the committed freeze file, run once. A failed look spends the hold-back window; the discovery window is not reopened for the same candidates (section 4 step 4). The result is descriptive; the forward confirmation of section 4 step 5 follows.
