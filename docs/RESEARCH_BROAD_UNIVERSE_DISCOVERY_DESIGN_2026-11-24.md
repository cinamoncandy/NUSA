# Broad-universe discovery and a longer forward confirmation (design, revised after independent review, 2026-11-24)

Status: design proposal for the owner's order of 2026-11-24 ("제안 순서 다 해"; this approves the **order of the design only**: every step below, the fetch included, is its own reviewed PR and needs the owner's go). Docs only. Nothing is fetched, read or run by this document. No gate, threshold, limit, capital or authority changes; a result only ever makes a rule a candidate for a later, separately pre-registered **spread-adjusted tradability test** (as in the RET48 pre-registration), never an order.

## 0. In plain words
Past charts can only suggest candidates; a verdict needs data that played no part in the choice. This plan uses many markets to find candidates faster and then confirms on new data. It will probably end with **"no candidate"**, which is an acceptable answer. The confirmation takes about **six months**, not weeks, because all markets move together and the number of independent observations is much smaller than the number of trades.

## 1. Why, and limits
- The 2023-2024 look on BTC, ETH, SOL and NEAR was spent (LOOK_FAIL); their forward test (`docs/RESEARCH_PREREG_2026-11-24_FORWARD_CONFIRMATION_RET48_H24.md`, decision 2028-03-31) stands untouched.
- Breadth adds candidate supply and trades, but not as much independent information as the trade count suggests: with pairwise correlations of 0.4 to 0.6 the effective sample is of the order of the number of independent 72-hour blocks (about 180 in 18 months), not the number of trades.
- After the 0.10 percent fee and a real spread few long-only spot rules survive; small markets cannot be traded at the modelled cost; delisted markets are missing from today's list (survivorship); the result is a **candle-close test**: entry at the next hourly close is not an executable price (stale last trade, bid-ask bounce).

The idea class (momentum with a 24 hour hold) is **not blind** after the H2 look. The historical part is therefore **discovery only**; the verdict comes from the forward test (section 7). The 2023-07 .. 2024-10 span of the unseen markets is a **descriptive hold-back that can veto, never confirm**.

## 3. Universe (frozen by metadata and a list, not by returns)
- Source: Upbit `GET /v1/market/all` (KRW markets, about 295 today) with the exact list and its SHA-256 committed first. Stable coins are excluded.
- **Listing rule (one rule):** a market enters a window 30 days after its listing date. The listing date comes from a **probe** that pages the market's daily candles back with `to` until the first one (a few extra requests per market, metadata only, no value used); it is committed with the list. Markets listed before the window start are treated as listed.
- **Tradability filter, fixed before any return is read and computed only from the discovery window** (the same list is then used for the hold-back and frozen for the forward test, so no hold-back or forward data selects markets): median tick / median close at most 10 bp and median daily traded value at least 1 billion KRW. The tick tiers are taken from Upbit's published table and **committed with their source before the filter is applied**; traded value is the API's `candle_acc_trade_price` (quote value), which the new fetch script stores. The counts passing each filter are published. The 10 bp tick bar removes most low-priced markets (XRP at 400 to 900 KRW in 2022-2023 would fail): **no market, XRP and the other study markets included, is exempt**. **Go/no-go gate:** step (2) of section 6 (metadata only, no candle value) must show a **forward pool of at least 40 markets after the exclusions of section 7** (about 5 trades per market and month over 6 months is then about 1,200 trades, the floor of section 7). If fewer pass, the plan stops with the report **"broad route not viable at this tradability bar"**; a looser filter may be pre-registered instead, but only from counts, before any candle is fetched, with its cost consequence stated; the trade floor of section 7 may be lowered to what the pool supports with the power consequence written down. No fetch or tooling PR starts before the gate is passed.
- **Cost model (optimistic by construction):** round trip fee 0.10 percent plus one tick over price as a spread floor; stress = fee 0.30 percent plus twice the floor. Momentum entries happen when spreads widen and no slippage is modelled; the forward test adds a **descriptive** spread-adjusted view from the host's order-book samples (retention 28 days, checked first).
- **Survivorship is declared, not fixed:** every historical result is labelled upward biased. Survivorship inflates both sides (survivors had positive drift). **LOW-side rules (buying after a drop) are excluded from the candidates** because dropped-then-delisted markets are exactly the ones missing, so their bias is the largest; HIGH-side results carry the same upward-bias label and only the forward test, which has no survivorship (section 7), decides. Keeping HIGH only leaves 64 of the 128 rules; the Bonferroni alpha uses only the shortlist size, as in the frozen logic.

## 4. Discovery (historical, descriptive)
- Grid: the **unchanged** 128-rule grid of `scripts/run-history-mining.js`, entry at the next hourly close; only HIGH-side rules can become candidates.
- Window: 2021-12-20 .. 2023-06-30, pooled over the tradable markets (the seen markets only if they pass the filter, declared).
- **Primary excess is time-matched:** the baseline of a trade at hour t is the mean net return of the always-in entry at the same hour t over **all tradable markets with a usable bar at t** (same 24 hour hold and costs), not a per-market time average; the per-market baseline stays descriptive. The baseline is **leave-one-out** (it excludes the trading market itself) and an hour counts only if at least **10** tradable markets have a usable entry and exit bar; hours below that are skipped, counted and never used for trades. This removes the common market factor ("the whole market was momentum-friendly that day").
- **Bootstrap:** time blocks over whole hours with all markets of an hour kept together; the decision p-value is the **maximum over block lengths 24, 72 and 168** (a 24 hour hold and a 48 hour signal make block 24 anti-conservative).
- **Candidate conditions (all required; otherwise `NO_CANDIDATE` by rule):** at least 100 trades; base net mean above 0; stress net mean above 0; time-matched excess above 0 with the max-block p below the Bonferroni alpha (0.05 over the shortlist size); both halves of the window positive **on both the net mean and the time-matched excess**; at most 5 rules, trade overlap limit as in the frozen logic. The cross-market sign table (excess, with at least 30 trades per market counted, weighted by trades) is **descriptive and not independent evidence**: with a common factor, "60 percent of markets positive" says little.
- Raw return thresholds pooled over markets fire mostly in high-volatility small coins; the discovered rule is therefore **not the same rule** as RET48 on the majors. A volatility-normalised signal would change the grid and is not done here.
- **Effective sample:** before the forward pre-registration the design requires an effective-N and minimum-detectable-effect estimate by block-bootstrap simulation on the discovery data.
- Output: shortlist, thresholds (pooled discovery quantiles), counts and per-market usable hours; no price printed; the freeze record is committed before anything else.
- **Secondary hold-back (descriptive veto only):** 2023-07-05 .. 2024-10-31 over the tradable markets that are not BTC, ETH, SOL, NEAR or XRP; run once, after the freeze record, with a spent-once guard; a negative time-matched excess drops the rule from the forward pre-registration.

## 5. Tooling (separate reviewed PRs, none reads a value)
1. **New file `scripts/run-broad-mining.js`** requiring the pure functions of `scripts/run-history-mining.js` and adding its own market list, per-market cost, same-hour baseline, sign tables, its own `GRID_FINGERPRINT`, pass-rule id and study name. **`scripts/run-history-mining.js` is not edited**: the RET48 pre-registration imports its unchanged functions and the spent H2 freeze record (`dc65065c...`) and look workflow pin its hash; a test fails if its hash or fingerprint changes. The script aggregates per rule instead of keeping every trade (an estimated 8 to 9 million trade objects would not fit the 512 MB heap).
2. **New fetch script** (market-list input, quote value stored, per-market exclusion with a committed reason list instead of aborting the whole run, disk precheck, stop after three consecutive HTTP 429 instead of retrying), synthetic tests only.
3. **Where it runs: on a GitHub-hosted runner, not on the PAPER host.** The data is public and needs no secret. The host shares its IP with the live collector and gap-fill (the Upbit quota is per IP and group; repeated 429 can ban the IP) and its disk was 90 percent full on 2026-11-18, and the cross-market training week and holdout (2026-11-21 .. 2026-12-10) must not be disturbed: **no broad fetch or discover run on the host before 2026-12-10**, and none later unless the host is shown to have the headroom. Runs are split into market groups of at most about 40 markets; about 25,000 requests at about 0.3 s each, twice for the reproduction check, is roughly 4 to 6 hours in total. Output is hashes and counts only; files are deleted at the end.
   Before the fetch PR a **no-data probe** (a `/v1/market/all` and ticker call from a hosted runner, no candle value) checks reachability and the quota behaviour; the fetch honours the `Remaining-Req` header (sleeps when the per-second budget is 0) in addition to the 150 ms spacing, is **resumable per group with a manifest per group**, and a group that stops on 429 is simply repeated later. GitHub-hosted runners share rotating IPs with other tenants, so random 429 stops are expected and are not failures of the plan. If the hosted IP proves unusable the fetch moves to the host **after 2026-12-10**. `node:sqlite` needs Node 22.5 or newer: the workflow would use the first-party `actions/setup-node` pinned by commit SHA (a stated deviation from the "no third-party action" line of the host workflows) or avoid SQLite.
4. The discover workflow, the freeze record and the descriptive hold-back run follow the existing pattern (dispatch-only, hash-pinned scripts, whole-value input checks, token unset before unpinned code).

## 7. Forward confirmation (to be pre-registered after step 6)
- **Disjoint from the RET48 test:** the broad forward universe **excludes BTC, ETH, SOL, NEAR, XRP and DOGE** (all markets of the other pre-registered studies) and reads none of their values; the forward market list is a **separate rule on a metadata snapshot at the pre-registration date** (Upbit market list with warning flags, the ticker's price and 24 hour traded value, the tick table; the same 10 bp tick and 1 billion KRW bars and the 30 day listing age), **not** the discovery-window list and not candle values after the 2026-11-20 cut. New listings after the discovery window may enter if they pass; the discovery-window filter applies to the discovery pool only. Forward fetches never include the six excluded markets for any post-cut span. The RET48 test, the cross-market study and signal screen V2 keep their own reading conventions untouched, and the two forward tests share no alpha because their markets and rules are disjoint.
- Rules: the survivors of section 4 (HIGH side), thresholds frozen from discovery; Bonferroni over the shortlist; gatekeeping order frozen.
- Data: Upbit public 60m candles fetched on the decision date and hash-checked (closed bars are immutable); no value, signal or trade count read before it.
- **One fixed calendar decision date, 26 weeks after the span start**, no early look, no optional stopping; a minimum of 1,000 resolved trades **and** a minimum effective sample from the pre-registered block-bootstrap estimate, otherwise INCONCLUSIVE. Power and the minimum detectable effect are computed by simulation on the discovery data and written into the pre-registration; they are expected to be modest (a true excess of 0.3 percentage points is unlikely to be detected), so a PASS in one regime is **not** evidence for another regime and a regime-descriptive table is part of the result.
- **Listing events:** a market under an exchange warning or delisting notice at the entry hour is not entered; an open position in a market that is delisted is closed at the last available close and counted (no dropping); an exit hour without a candle uses the last prior close and is counted, never dropped (drops would be optimistic: exit holes correlate with crashes).
- A pass makes the rules candidates for a **spread-adjusted tradability test** only.

## 8. Expected outcomes, stated honestly
- Most likely: no rule meets the conditions of section 4, so the answer is "no candidate", reached in days to weeks of compute, not months.
- Possible: one or two rules survive discovery and the veto; the forward test then needs about six months and may well be INCONCLUSIVE.
- Not possible by this route: a "verified strategy" from past data alone, or a fast verdict.
