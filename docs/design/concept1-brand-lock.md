# NUSA Concept 1 Brand Lock

Final production symbol direction:

- upper geometric triangular form
- lower horizontal horizon/reflection form
- no beacon, sun rays, resort imagery, literal temple, palm, volcano, or crypto-token motif
- monochrome-safe and small-size legible
- Android launcher and in-app mark must share the same Concept 1 geometry

The concept is inspired by Balinese principles of balance, upper/lower correspondence, island/sea relationship, order, repetition, and stability rather than literal tourism imagery.
