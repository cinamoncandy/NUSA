## 1. Why, and the prior (stated before any result)
- The close-based single-market rule families (SMA, RSI, Donchian) on KRW-XRP are exhausted by the policy (811 experiment records, 0 candidates on 2026-11-19). A family that **ranks markets against each other** is a different hypothesis, and it was untestable because candles existed for one market only. Since the release of the research-market widening (target 2026-11-19), candles are collected for KRW-XRP, KRW-BTC, KRW-ETH, KRW-SOL, KRW-DOGE.
- Honest prior: **low.** Cross-sectional momentum at hour scale on five liquid markets must beat 0.05 percent per side fees plus spread; trade flow failed once at this horizon (2026-10-13) and order-book/ML families have not passed. Five markets are few, so the ranking is noisy. The study is registered because a clean negative is also a result, and because it is an untried family that needs candles for several markets (order books for these markets are already collected).
- No candle value of KRW-BTC, ETH, SOL or DOGE was ever read before this draft. KRW-XRP values were examined by earlier research, but its values of the span below lie in the future, so nothing about the span is known.

## 2. Universe and eligibility
- Markets: KRW-XRP, KRW-BTC, KRW-ETH, KRW-SOL, KRW-DOGE (fixed; not extended during this study).
- Bars: 60-minute bars built from stored 1-minute candles, decision times at every full UTC hour. A bar counts only if all of its 60 one-minute closes are present or proven exchange-absent (the existing absence proof); an incomplete bar is missing.
- **Usable order book** at time t: the last order-book sample in (t - 2 min, t] with best bid > 0, best ask > 0 and best bid < best ask (not crossed). **Spread** of a decision hour: (best ask - best bid) / mid, in bp, mid = (ask + bid) / 2.
- A market is eligible for the study if, in the training part, at least 90 percent of decision hours have a complete bar **and** a usable order book at the decision hour, and the **median over the training decision hours that have a usable book** of the spread is at most 20 bp. A hour in which a market has no complete bar or no usable book makes that market ineligible for that hour only (not for the whole study).
- A decision hour in which fewer than 3 eligible markets have a complete bar and a book is skipped.

## 3. Rule (the hypothesis; one primary cell, no selection)
- Timing (no look-ahead): decision times are full UTC hours t. `close_i(t)` is the close of the one-minute candle whose **close time** is t in the candle store (the candle covering the minute that ends at t), whatever start-time convention the exchange API uses. The order book used at t is the **last sample in (t - 2 min, t]**; nothing recorded after t is used for the decision, and the exit uses the last sample in (t + H - 2 min, t + H].
- At decision hour t, for each eligible market i compute `r_i(L) = close_i(t) / close_i(t - L) - 1`. A market with a missing `close_i(t - L)` or `close_i(t)` is not eligible at that hour.
- Rank by `r_i(L)`; ties are broken by market-code order (alphabetical), as in the trade-flow study. **Go long the single top-ranked market** (spot only, long only, one position, no short, no leverage).
- Execution: buy at the best ask at t, sell at the best bid at t + H. A trade with no book at t or at t + H is dropped; a trade whose exit time t + H is **after** the end of its part is dropped; an exit exactly at the part end is kept. Drops are counted and reported with their reasons (no book at t, no book at the exit, exit after the part end); a trade dropped for a missing exit book is a mild selection effect and is disclosed.
- **Primary cell (the only PASS-eligible cell): L = 24 h, H = 1 h**, decisions every hour (non-overlapping because H = 1 h). Reason fixed now, before any value: the 1 h holding gives the most trades in a 10-day holdout (about 240), and the 24 h lookback is less noisy than 6 h. Nothing is selected on validation: with a 2-day validation part the 4 h cell would have about 12 trades, so a selection would be a coin flip and would only dilute power.
- **Descriptive cells (reported, never PASS-eligible):** (L 6 h, H 1 h), (L 6 h, H 4 h), (L 24 h, H 4 h). They are computed on the holdout with the same rules and listed with the primary cell so that the family is visible; their results can not make the study pass.
- Benchmarks at the same decision times, same execution and costs: (a) equal weight of the eligible markets; (b) KRW-BTC alone (a beta reference, **reported only, not a PASS condition**; hours in which KRW-BTC is not eligible or has no usable book at t or t + H are left out of this reference only).
- Costs: fee 0.05 percent per side. Stress: 2 x fee plus 5 bp per side.
- Reported, not tested: the share of trades per market (concentration), the market-by-market mean net, the gross mean before costs.

## 5. Power, multiple comparisons and the PASS rule
- **Power (assumptions, not measured data):** a top-1 trade sd of about 0.8 percent per hour and a total cost near 0.15 percent per round trip including spread. For the primary cell with about 240 trades the standard error of the mean is about 0.05 percent, so the critical mean net is about 0.12 percent per trade, and 80 percent power needs a true net edge of about 0.16 percent per trade (about 0.3 percent gross per hour). A realistic edge of 0 to 0.05 percent net has **under 5 percent chance to PASS**. This study can detect a **large** edge only. A FAIL is a clean negative about large edges and says **nothing** about small ones; it is not a test of whether a small edge exists. The study is registered knowing this.
- **Stage 1, signal existence (gross, before costs; it uses candles only, no bid or ask):** for every decision hour t with at least 3 eligible markets that have `close_i(t - 24 h)`, `close_i(t)` and `close_i(t + 1 h)` (same close-time convention), compute the Spearman rank correlation (average ranks for ties) between `r_i(24 h)` and the next-hour return `close_i(t + 1 h) / close_i(t) - 1`. Hours that fail the condition are excluded. The statistic is the mean of these hourly values over the holdout. It is expected to be more sensitive than a top-1 trade test (not quantified) and is **required for PASS**: no gross signal, no pass.
- **Bootstrap definition (Stage 1 and the trade-mean test of Stage 2):** a **circular block bootstrap** on the time-ordered series (hourly IC values; trade net returns in trade order), **block length 24** (24 hours for the hourly series; 24 consecutive trade indices for the trades, so blocks of trades are non-contiguous in time where trades were dropped), B = 10,000 resamples. The null is imposed by **centring**: the series is demeaned (mean subtracted), resampled, and the resample means `m*_b` are compared with the observed mean `m`: one-sided `p = (1 + #{b : m*_b >= m}) / (B + 1)`. Random numbers: **splitmix64** seeded with the integer 20261119 produces the four 64-bit state words of **xoshiro256\*\***; a uniform `u` is the top 53 bits of an output divided by 2^53; a block start index is `floor(u * n)`.
- **Stage 2, tradable edge (the primary cell, after costs):** all of the following on the holdout: at least 50 trades (the 1 h cell has about 240); mean net per trade > 0 under the fee costs and under the stress costs; one-sided Student t test on the trade net returns p < 0.0125 **and** the circular block bootstrap of the trade mean (as defined above) p < 0.0125; the **paired** difference (selected trade net minus the equal-weight benchmark net at the same decision hour) has a mean > 0 with a **block sign-flip permutation** p < 0.0125: signs are flipped for whole non-overlapping blocks of 24 consecutive trade indices (a final shorter block is one block), 10,000 draws, the same PRNG, `p = (1 + #{b : m*_b >= m}) / (B + 1)` on the mean paired difference. The tests are on means; the clustering of differences is the reason for blocks and the permutation is still an approximation. Absolute and paired tests are an intersection, hence conservative. The KRW-BTC reference is reported, not required.
- Hypothesis count: K = 4 is counted for the family (the primary and the three descriptive cells) and the threshold 0.05 / 4 = 0.0125 is used for every test above although only one cell is PASS-eligible. This is a separate family and dataset, registered like the trade-flow and order-book studies, not part of the runtime experiment ledger or its cumulative holdout bar.
- `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` if fewer than 3 markets are eligible or the primary cell has fewer than 50 trades. The rules are not changed and the span is not re-run.
- A PASS is recorded as eligible for a SHADOW forward test only (a later, separately pre-registered span); it is never promotion evidence and changes no gate, limit or authority.
