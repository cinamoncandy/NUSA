# NUSA Investment Performance Engine Non-Goals

This roadmap does not authorize or imply:

- guaranteed returns
- automatic LIVE activation
- real-money order authority
- withdrawal or transfer authority
- AI-controlled Risk limits
- strategy promotion based on one backtest
- deletion of adverse evidence
- data snooping through future information
- replacement of human/environment evidence required by WO-0051

The roadmap exists to improve falsification quality and research evidence before any production transition is considered.
