# L1 candidate supply: widen the research markets (design, 2026-11-19)

Status: DESIGN, revised after an independent review (P0 corrected in section 1). Owner answer 2026-11-19: "add 4 markets (recommended)" with the condition that only research widens and orders, limits and authority stay as they are. No code, host or release change here. PAPER_ONLY, `liveAuthority=NONE`, `productionMutationAllowed=false`, `aiAuthority=ZERO_AUTHORITY`, orders OFF.

## 1. Why (from the repository and the host)

- All research runs on one market. `deploy/oracle/paper-markets.json` holds `["KRW-XRP"]` (owner decision 2026-10-03, "XRP first"). One 1-minute candle series exists on the host (`dataIntegrity.candleSeries 1`, 81206 candles). Order-book and trade samples are already collected for about 300 markets, so the data the cross-market ideas need on the quote side is there; candles are not.
- The meta-learning policy marks `CLOSE + INTRADAY/DAILY + RULE + FIVE (at most five markets)` as EXHAUSTED (`researchMetaLearning.ts`). Adding markets to the same three rule families (SMA, RSI, Donchian) is not expected to produce candidates. Widening the markets only pays off together with a **new hypothesis family** that needs several markets (cross-market relative momentum, item 3 of `docs/RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS_DIVERSITY_PLAN_20261112.md`).
- **Correction after the independent review (P0).** An earlier draft of this document said the experiment runner would not run the exhausted families on new markets. That is wrong. The policy only reorders work: `metaLearningSteering` sets `longBarTickEvery` to 4 for the 15m and 60m orchestrators and "nothing is ever stopped" (`researchMetaLearning.ts:150-170`); the 1m orchestrator always ticks, and `researchExperimentOrchestrator.ts:479` loops every variant over **every** configured market, each with its own experiment family id, dataset id and trial count. Putting five markets in `NUSA_RESEARCH_MARKETS` would therefore start the SMA, RSI and Donchian grids on four more markets (about five times the experiments per tick), consume new holdout slots, add configurations to K and make the cumulative holdout bar (`0.05 / configurations`) stricter, with no pre-registration. That must not happen as a side effect of a data step.

So this is two steps, and only the second can create a candidate. Step A must collect candles without starting experiments on them.

## 2. Step A: data (this design)

1. **Separate research markets from the PAPER feed markets.** Today one file sets both `NUSA_CLOUD_UPBIT_MARKETS` (the PAPER trading feed) and `NUSA_RESEARCH_MARKETS` (`scripts/start-cloud-runtime.js`, lines about 170-178). Putting four more markets in that file would also widen the PAPER trading feed, which the owner did not ask for. Proposal: an optional `researchMarkets` field in the owner file, applied only to `NUSA_RESEARCH_MARKETS`; absent means the old behaviour (the `markets` list for both). The same limits stay: 1-5 distinct KRW markets, fail closed on a malformed file. A test pins that `NUSA_CLOUD_UPBIT_MARKETS` stays `KRW-XRP`.
2. **Collect and experiment are separate switches (required by the P0 above).** Candles are collected and backfilled for the research markets; experiments run only on an explicit `experimentMarkets` list that stays `["KRW-XRP"]` (a subset of the research markets, XRP first) until the step B pre-registration is merged. This needs a code change in the research composition and orchestrator (the experiment loop reads `experimentMarkets`, backfill and gap-fill read the research markets), with tests that pin: five collected markets produce zero experiments, evaluations or holdout use on the four new markets; the experiment count per tick and the configuration count in the ledger are unchanged; an `experimentMarkets` entry that is not a research market fails closed. The gate must be built as follows (second independent review, P1): the orchestrator receives `experimentMarkets` as its `markets` option, not the five research markets, because that option is also embedded in every session's `datasetId` (`upbit-1m-closed:${markets.join(",")}:${interval}`) and in `deterministicConfig.markets` (`researchExperimentOrchestrator.ts` about lines 281, 287, 464, 470). Filtering only the loop would change the session and pre-commit identity and the configuration hash of the existing XRP experiments. A test pins that the XRP session `datasetId` and `deterministicConfig` are byte-identical before and after the change. Backfill, gap-fill and the backfill-done check (`composition.ts` about 363, 399, 465) read the research markets; `closedLearningProductionRuntime.ts:479` (the meta-learning universe input) reads the experiment list. Without this gate, step A must not be released.
2b. **Markets:** `KRW-XRP` (existing series stays, nothing is rebuilt), `KRW-BTC`, `KRW-ETH`, `KRW-SOL`, `KRW-DOGE` (five, the launcher's maximum). Liquid KRW markets with long histories; the list is the owner's to change.
3. **Backfill, not waiting:** the existing gap-fill fills candle history from the exchange's public candle endpoint with a bounded rate (`research-gap-fill ... pages=8` per pass on 2026-11-19). Four more markets need about 56 days of 1-minute candles each (about 80,000 rows each, 400 pages of 200). The time this takes at the current bounded rate is NOT measured; the first release must watch it and may not raise the rate.

## 3. Load and disk: what is known and what is not

- Known: a manifest build for one market took about 8 s per interval (1m, 15m, 60m) on 2026-11-19, now in bounded steps that hand the thread back (collector max stall 1.2 s). Five markets means about five times the builds per experiment tick.
- Known: 1-minute candles are small (one market is about 81,000 rows of a 2.3 GB database dominated by 5.2 million order-book rows). Four more markets add on the order of 320,000 candle rows.
- Also scales with the market count and was not in the first draft: backfill and gap-fill loop over all markets (`researchExperimentComposition.ts` about lines 363 and 399) with per-market progress; manifest builds are per market and per interval (1m, 15m, 60m); a gap-fill pass blocks the experiment tick, which retries every second while a backfill or gap-fill is active. With the experiment gate above, only collection, backfill and gap-fill grow, not the experiments.
- Order matters: strategies, `collectionProgress`, the order-book coverage watcher and the trade-tick coverage watcher all use the first market (`composition.ts:243`, `orchestrator.ts:188`, `runtime.ts:844`, `runtime.ts:847`). `KRW-XRP` must stay first in both lists; a test pins it.
- NOT known: the CPU and wall-clock effect on the host, the disk headroom after the release backup (the root disk was 90 percent full on 2026-11-18; the diagnose of 2026-11-19 showed 2.3 GB runner, 578 MB logs, 188 MB releases), and the gap-fill duration. The first release after approval is therefore staged with a recorded before/after read (`eventLoopMaxStallMs`, `researchGapFill` timings, disk) and the owner can switch the field back in a reviewed release.

## 4. Statistical accounting (must be fixed before any result)

More markets with the same rule grids add configurations. The cumulative holdout bar is `0.05 / (all configurations so far + the next experiment's)` (meta-learning rule 5), so every added configuration makes the bar stricter. Therefore:
- Step A, with the experiment gate of section 2, adds **no new configuration and consumes no holdout**: the exhausted families keep running on KRW-XRP only. It only collects candles and keeps the data ready. Without the gate it would add configurations and tighten the bar (section 1, correction).
- Step B must be pre-registered before its code is written: the signal (cross-market relative momentum rank over the five markets), horizon, grid, training/validation/holdout periods, cost model, the exact K, the pass rule, and whether the five markets are tested as one pooled hypothesis (one test, K counts once) or five separate ones (K counts five). Recommendation: one pooled cross-sectional hypothesis per grid cell; the pre-registration, not this document, decides. No gate, threshold or holdout rule is relaxed, and the holdout is opened once.
