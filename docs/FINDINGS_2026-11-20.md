- Streak: 14 consecutive identical decisions, **6 more are needed** to reach 20. How many of the counted merges touched workflow or release paths was **not checked** (unknown; the rule needs at least 3).

The shadow has never been shown a HOLD or a global freeze, which are the only things the Worker contributes that the shadow reads from `config/release-control.json`. Matching CLEAR on a fully clear day is weaker evidence than matching a HOLD. S3 (removing the Worker call) must not start on this count alone.

## 2. Order-book coverage (68.2 percent against the 90 percent bar of the order-book study)
Source: public `/health` `orderBookCoverage` and `apps/cloud/src/orderBookCoverage.ts`; the host database was not read.
- Window: the last 192 hours; 131 covered, 61 uncovered, longest gap 21 hours. An hour is covered when a stored sample lies in the 2 minutes up to the full hour. The watch looks at one market (the first of the research list); the collector requests all KRW markets together every minute, so the other markets share the same gaps.
- Cause (from the code, not from the data): the collector runs once a minute, so a gap needs the collector to be stopped or failing around the full hour. A restart of a few minutes uncovers an hour only if it spans the minutes just before the hour; long gaps (21 hours) are outages. A 192-hour window means old outages leave the window by themselves.
- Estimate (stated as an estimate: the positions of the gaps were not read): reaching 90 percent needs 173 covered hours, that is at most 19 uncovered. If the 61 uncovered hours are mostly before 2026-11-16 (the controller installs and restarts), they leave the window by about 2026-11-24, and each release adds only a few percent of an hour of risk. **Open question: where the 61 hours lie**; a read-only per-hour listing from the host would answer it.
- Consequence for releases: every service restart costs coverage risk. The release freeze of 2026-11-24 to 2026-11-28 helps. A new order-book study needs a **new pre-registration** after gap-free collection (the existing one ended INSUFFICIENT_DATA); nothing is changed in the old rule.
- Not done and not proposed here: no gap is repaired, no study rule changes.
