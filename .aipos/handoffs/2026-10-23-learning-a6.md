## First broken transition

The old in-memory `ExplorationRegistry` trusted caller-supplied experiment/family/configuration identities and rehydrated arbitrary verdict/counter objects. Its allotment reset with each process, and competing writers could make decisions from the same stale history.

## Change

- A6 now requires a durable A2 fingerprint and derives its cycle from the stored Learning Input Set.
- Candidate shape is validated by the existing Research Candidate contract and exact strategy-version membership is checked with the existing `StrategyFamilyRegistry`.
- Configuration and near-duplicate identities are derived from a canonical non-secret descriptor; caller-selected identity strings are removed.
- Assessment receipts use the existing Evolution Learning SQLite hash-chain ledger. Cycle budget and family rules are recomputed inside its serialized append validation; SQLite restart/replay is idempotent and stale concurrent assessment fails closed.
- `ALLOTTED` remains evaluation permission only and retains PIT/OOS/walk-forward/cost/sample/qualification/Governance gates.
- No production caller, scheduler, candidate, qualification, Governance, PAPER activation, or authority was added.
